## Search Query Qualification Rules

- Prioritize phrases that indicate an active requirement, a request for recommendations, or a clearly stated business problem.
- Treat general educational questions as lower-intent until additional context establishes a business need.
- Distinguish buyers from recruiters, job seekers, freelancers, and agencies offering their own services.
- Do not classify a person as a lead based on a keyword match alone.
- Evaluate the complete post and relevant discussion before deciding whether to engage.
- Match the prospect's need to an approved The Digital Frog service.
- Avoid repeated outreach to the same person across multiple platforms.
- Respect platform rules, privacy expectations, and explicit requests not to be contacted.
- Use public content for relevant discovery, not for spam or deceptive personalization.

## Final Rule

Search for buying signals, not just service keywords.

Understand the problem before suggesting a solution.

Qualify the prospect before promoting The Digital Frog.

Relevance comes before outreach.

# Important

Never copy these examples word-for-word repeatedly.

Use them as behavioral examples.

The final response must always be generated from the actual post and conversation context.

The agent must distinguish between:

- A person buying a service
- A company recruiting an employee
- A freelancer or agency offering services
- A person seeking employment
- A person discussing a topic generally
- A person asking about the founder
- A person asking about the company
- An unrelated post

A keyword match alone does not establish buying intent.

SEO, AEO, GEO, AI automation, paid advertising, website development, and other services should only be introduced when relevant to the person's actual requirements.

Do not guarantee search rankings, AI citations, lead volume, conversion rates, or revenue outcomes.

Prioritize natural conversation, accurate classification, and genuine business relevance over promotion.

# Lead Qualification Stages

## Purpose

The lead qualification system identifies potential customers from publicly accessible social media posts and conversations.

The agent must determine whether a person has a genuine business need, whether that need matches The Digital Frog's services, and how ready the person is to discuss a potential project.

A keyword match alone does not qualify someone as a lead.

## Stage 0 — Irrelevant

Definition:

The post has no meaningful connection to The Digital Frog's services or target business opportunities.

Examples:

- Personal updates
- Entertainment posts
- General discussions unrelated to the company's services
- Unrelated requests for help
- Posts with coincidental keyword matches

Classification:

IRRELEVANT

Lead Status:

DISQUALIFIED

Action:

IGNORE

## Stage 1 — Potential Intent

Definition:

The post mentions a possible business problem or need, but there is insufficient evidence of an actual purchasing requirement.

Examples:

- "We need to improve our online presence."
- "How can a business get more leads?"
- "AI automation is becoming important for startups."
- "Does anyone have experience with SEO?"

Classification:

POTENTIAL_INTENT

Lead Status:

UNQUALIFIED

Action:

Monitor the context or seek clarification if a natural conversation is appropriate.

Do not immediately pitch The Digital Frog.

## Stage 2 — Relevant Need Identified

Definition:

The person expresses a recognizable need that matches one or more actual The Digital Frog services, but their intention to purchase remains unclear.

Examples:

- "Our website needs improvement."
- "We want to explore GEO for our brand."
- "We are considering automating our lead follow-ups."
- "We need to improve our Google Ads performance."

Classification:

NEED_IDENTIFIED

Lead Status:

UNQUALIFIED

Action:

Identify the specific problem and determine whether the person is exploring options, seeking advice, or considering hiring a service provider.

## Stage 3 — Qualified Potential Lead

Definition:

There is credible evidence that the person or business may purchase a relevant service.

The requirement must match The Digital Frog's capabilities, and the post must contain more than a generic keyword or vague expression of interest.

Qualification signals may include:

- Explicitly seeking an agency or service provider
- Asking for recommendations for a relevant service
- Requesting proposals or quotations
- Describing a business problem and asking for professional help
- Looking for an outsourced solution that matches the company's services

Examples:

- "Can anyone recommend an agency for B2B SEO?"
- "We're looking for someone to build our SaaS landing page."
- "We need help setting up AI-powered customer support."
- "Looking for a GEO agency to improve our brand's AI search visibility."

Classification:

QUALIFIED_POTENTIAL_LEAD

Lead Status:

QUALIFIED

Action:

Prepare a personalized, context-aware response.

Do not assume the person has a budget, decision-making authority, or immediate timeline unless there is evidence.

## Stage 4 — Engaged Lead

Definition:

The person has responded to an initial interaction or entered a relevant conversation about their business need.

Examples:

- They explain their current marketing setup.
- They describe their website or automation requirements.
- They share the challenges they are facing.
- They ask relevant questions about a potential solution.

Classification:

ENGAGED_LEAD

Lead Status:

ACTIVE

Action:

Understand the requirement, scope, current setup, desired outcome, and potential next steps.

Introduce The Digital Frog naturally when its services are relevant.

## Stage 5 — Sales-Qualified Lead

Definition:

The prospect has a sufficiently clear business requirement and demonstrates credible intent to evaluate a paid solution.

Qualification should consider:

- **Need:** Is there a specific problem The Digital Frog can solve?
- **Fit:** Does the requirement match the company's services?
- **Authority:** Is the person a decision-maker or involved in the purchasing process?
- **Budget:** Is there an established or plausible budget for the project?
- **Timeline:** Is there a known timeframe for starting?
- **Intent:** Is the prospect willing to discuss a proposal, consultation, or project scope?

Not every detail must be known at the first conversation, but unknown information must remain marked as unknown.

Classification:

SALES_QUALIFIED_LEAD

Lead Status:

SALES_READY

Action:

Move toward a discovery call, requirements discussion, proposal, or other appropriate sales step.

## Stage 6 — Opportunity

Definition:

The prospect and The Digital Frog are actively discussing a defined potential engagement.

Examples:

- A discovery call has been agreed upon.
- The project scope is being discussed.
- The prospect has requested a proposal.
- Pricing, deliverables, or implementation requirements are being evaluated.

Classification:

OPPORTUNITY

Lead Status:

IN_PIPELINE

Action:

Track the requirements, next action, relevant stakeholders, timeline, and agreed follow-up.

A proposal request is not the same as a closed deal.

## Stage 7 — Won or Lost

### Won

Definition:

The prospect has formally agreed to proceed under the applicable commercial terms.

Classification:

CLOSED_WON

Lead Status:

CUSTOMER

Action:

Proceed with onboarding and delivery according to the agreed scope.

### Lost

Definition:

The opportunity will not proceed, or the prospect has explicitly declined.

Classification:

CLOSED_LOST

Lead Status:

DISQUALIFIED_OR_CLOSED

Action:

Record the reason when known, such as no budget, no current need, unsuitable requirements, competitor selected, or no decision.

Do not continue promotional outreach after an explicit refusal.

## Disqualification Rules

The following must not be classified as qualified sales leads solely because of matching keywords:

### Recruiters

Examples:

- "We're hiring an SEO specialist."
- "Looking for a full-time web developer."
- "Join our digital marketing team."

Classification:

RECRUITER

Action:

DISQUALIFY unless a separate outsourcing requirement is explicitly expressed.

### Job Seekers

Examples:

- "Open to work."
- "Looking for freelance marketing projects."
- "Seeking an AI automation role."

Classification:

JOB_SEEKER

Action:

DISQUALIFY

### Service Providers

Examples:

- "I'm a GEO consultant taking new clients."
- "I offer SEO services."
- "Available for website development projects."

Classification:

SERVICE_PROVIDER

Action:

DISQUALIFY as a buyer unless a separate genuine purchasing requirement is expressed.

### Unrelated Buyers

Example:

"I need a logo designer."

Classification:

BUYER_OUTSIDE_SERVICE_SCOPE

Action:

DISQUALIFY for standalone logo design.

### Ambiguous Posts

Example:

"Looking for a developer."

Classification:

UNKNOWN

Action:

Do not promote to qualified lead status without additional context.

## Important Qualification Rules

1. Qualification stages must be based on observable evidence, not assumptions.
2. A person mentioning SEO, AEO, GEO, advertising, AI, or websites is not automatically a buyer.
3. A person expressing a business problem is not automatically ready to purchase.
4. Do not invent budgets, decision-making authority, timelines, or purchasing intent.
5. Missing information must be recorded as UNKNOWN rather than assumed.
6. A lead may advance only when new evidence supports the next stage.
7. A person can be relevant to the company without being sales-qualified.
8. Public engagement does not automatically grant permission for repeated promotional DMs.
9. Respect explicit refusals and requests not to be contacted.
10. Do not classify a lead as won until an actual commercial agreement is confirmed.

## Recommended Lead Progression

IRRELEVANT → DISQUALIFIED

POTENTIAL_INTENT → NEED_IDENTIFIED → QUALIFIED_POTENTIAL_LEAD → ENGAGED_LEAD → SALES_QUALIFIED_LEAD → OPPORTUNITY → CLOSED_WON / CLOSED_LOST

The agent must be able to disqualify a lead at any stage if new information establishes that the requirement is irrelevant, unsuitable, or not a genuine buying opportunity.

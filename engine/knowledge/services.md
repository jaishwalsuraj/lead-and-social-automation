## Matching Rule

A keyword match is not sufficient.

The agent must determine:

1. What does the person actually need?
2. Is the person buying, requesting, or exploring a business solution?
3. Does the requirement match an approved The Digital Frog service?
4. Is the person instead recruiting an employee?
5. Is the person offering their own services?
6. Is the person seeking employment or freelance work?
7. Is the topic unrelated to The Digital Frog?
8. Is there enough context to classify the opportunity confidently?

Only genuine buying or project intent with a relevant service match should qualify as a potential The Digital Frog lead.

If intent is unclear, classify the post as potential or unqualified rather than assuming the person is ready to buy.

## Final Rule

Understand the requirement before matching the service.

Match the service before introducing the agency.

Qualify the prospect before initiating a sales conversation.

Relevance comes before promotion.

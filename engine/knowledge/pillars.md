# Content Pillars for Automated Social Publishing

This file defines the strategic content pillars used by The Digital Frog's automated social publishing engine.

The automation cycles through these pillars to generate fresh, thought-provoking discussions, educational posts, quote cards, and 5-slide carousel decks.

The content is designed to attract potential clients, build authority, educate business owners, and start relevant conversations around digital marketing, technology, AI automation, and business growth.

The publishing engine should prioritize practical insights, real business problems, actionable solutions, and meaningful discussions over generic motivational content or aggressive promotion.

## Publishing Guidelines

- Rotate content across all six pillars to maintain variety.
- Generate practical discussions, educational posts, quote cards, and 5-slide carousel decks.
- Start with a recognizable business problem, misconception, mistake, or opportunity.
- Make each post useful to its intended audience, even when it does not directly promote a service.
- Use clear examples and actionable recommendations rather than vague motivational statements.
- Keep technical content understandable to the intended audience.
- Use relevant questions to encourage thoughtful discussion without engagement bait.
- Mention The Digital Frog only when its services are genuinely relevant to the topic.
- Mention Suraaj Jaiswal only when founder perspective or personal experience adds value.
- Do not invent client stories, case studies, performance metrics, revenue figures, or personal experiences.
- Do not promise guaranteed leads, revenue, rankings, AI visibility, or conversion improvements.
- Avoid publishing repetitive posts that merely rephrase the same idea.
- Keep the content aligned with the agency's approved services and target audiences.

## Content Distribution

The automation should rotate through the six pillars instead of publishing only about AI or digital marketing.

Each publishing cycle should consider:

- **Educational content**: Explain a concept, process, or common mistake.
- **Problem-focused content**: Identify a challenge the target audience recognizes.
- **Practical content**: Share a checklist, framework, workflow, or implementation tip.
- **Opinion-led content**: Present a reasoned viewpoint that encourages genuine discussion.
- **Founder-led content**: Share relevant lessons or perspectives grounded in actual experience.
- **Service-relevant content**: Explain a problem The Digital Frog can help solve without turning every post into a sales pitch.

## Final Rule

Educate before promoting.

Solve problems before pitching services.

Build trust before asking for business.

Every post should deliver value to its intended audience while strengthening The Digital Frog's positioning in digital marketing, technology, and AI-powered business automation.
